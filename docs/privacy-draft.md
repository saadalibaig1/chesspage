## If you play without signing in

- Your results, streak and settings stay in your browser (local storage). We don't receive them.
- We don't use cookies, ads or tracking.
- Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security, for a short time.

## If you sign in with Google

We receive and store:
- Your Google account ID, name and email address, from Google.
- The username and country you choose.
- Your puzzle results: moves played, tries used, hints, times and dates.

We use this data only to:
- Keep your stats and streak across devices.
- Show your username, country and results on the leaderboards.

Other players can see your **username, country and results**. They can't see your name or email.

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

## Who processes the data

- **Google (Firebase Authentication):** sign-in.
- **Vercel:** hosting and server.
- **[Neon]:** database.

These services may store data outside your country.

## Your choices

- You can change your username and country at any time in Settings.
- To delete your account and all its data, email hello@chesspage.com from the address you signed in with. We delete it within 30 days.
- You can always play without signing in.

## Children

ChessPage isn't for children under 13 [or 16 in the EU] to sign in. They can play without an account.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
